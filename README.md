## Substitute Chaos

A browser-based deck-building card game where you play a substitute teacher trying to survive the school day. Fight classroom supplies with other classroom supplies until the final bell rings.

Built by [team names] for CS 3980 (AI as Software Development Teammate) at MSU Denver, Fall 2026.

## Play

Live at: `https://<username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`

Just open the link in a browser. No install, no login, no account.

## Concept

- Single-player deck builder inspired by Slay the Spire
- Turn-based combat against school-themed enemies (Rogue Staplers, Angry Chalkboards, Hall Monitors, etc.)
- Build a deck of school supplies as attacks and defenses
- Survive a run of fights, ending with a boss fight against The Principal

## Tech

Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
