How to Play
Substitute Chaos

Welcome, new teacher! Here's everything you need to know before your first day.

Your Goal

Deplete the Opponent's Sanity!

Welcome to substitute duty, teacher. Your first assignment is the notoriously bored and attention-seeking class clown. Think of their health as their Sanity. Use clever cards to drain away their enthusiasm.

Example Cards

Attack

Attack cards deal damage to your opponent's Sanity.

Defense

Defense cards let you restore your Sanity or protect it with Relaxation.

Skill

Skill cards provide you with energy, let you shuffle your discard pile back into your deck, or allow you to draw more cards.

Power

Power cards stay in play after you use them and trigger their effect again and again.

Hover over these example cards to see their tooltips and abilities in action.

Your Card

You'll start as a Substitute Teacher. Your Sanity represents how long you can keep teaching before losing it all.

Your Stats

The hero card displays your three main stats:

  • Sanity (🧠): Your health. When this drops to zero, you're unconscious.
  • Relaxation (😌): Damage block that reduces incoming harm each turn. Gain more by resting.
  • Coffee (☕): Energy to play cards. Spend coffee to use them!

Each card shows its cost in the top corner. Higher costs take more coffee but usually have greater impact.

Power Slots

Next to your field, Power Cards stay after you play them. Some effects even let you shuffle from deck when the enemy does!

Key Rules

  • Play cards at will: You don't have to wait your turn. Play any card you can afford.
  • Energy costs: Cards need coffee refills between turns. Watch your energy!
  • Relaxation blocks damage: Gain Relaxation each turn to temporarily block incoming damage.

Controls

Space Click a card or press Space/Enter to play it.
