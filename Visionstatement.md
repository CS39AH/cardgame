## Vision Statement

- **For** casual gamers, students, and fans of deck-building games[^deckbuilding]
- **Who** want a quick, funny, strategic game they can play anywhere without installing, buying, or signing up for anything
- **The** Substitute Chaos **is a** browser-based[^browser] deck-building card game
- **That** turns surviving a chaotic school day into a replayable run[^run] of tactical card battles[^battles] against sentient school supplies, where you pick one of six teachers, fight your way through six locations from Homeroom to the Principal's Office, and grow your deck with every win
- **Unlike** Slay the Spire[^sts] and other deck builders, which require a purchase and a download,
- **Our product** is free, runs in any modern browser, saves your progress automatically, and wraps real deck-building strategy in a lighthearted school setting everyone recognizes

[^deckbuilding]: **Deck-building game:** a card game where you start with a small, basic deck of cards and improve it as you play by adding stronger cards and removing weak ones. Building a good deck is the main strategy.

[^browser]: **Browser-based:** the game runs inside a web browser like Chrome, Safari, or Edge, just by visiting a link. Nothing to download or install.

[^run]: **Run:** one full playthrough from the start of the day until you win or lose. Each teacher plays differently and every reward is a choice, so the game can be replayed many times.

[^battles]: **Tactical card battles:** turn-based fights where you choose which cards to play each turn, deciding when to attack, when to defend, and how to use your limited energy.

[^sts]: **Slay the Spire:** a popular deck-building game released in 2019 that helped make the genre well known. It's the main inspiration for this project.
